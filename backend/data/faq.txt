# Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. International shipping rates and times will vary depending on your location.

Q: How can I track my order?
A: You can track your order status by looking up your order ID. If the order is shipped, you will see the shipping date and estimated arrival.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay.

Q: Do you offer a warranty on your electronics?
A: Yes, all our electronics come with a standard 1-year manufacturer warranty covering internal defects. Physical damage is not covered.
